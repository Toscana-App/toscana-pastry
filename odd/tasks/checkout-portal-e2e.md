- **C1 (S3/S4, p06 RF-P6-12/13/14) — Pagos Mercado Pago + efectivo (backend)**: `order` gana `payment_method ∈ {mercadopago, cash}` (requerido desde portal; default `cash` en admin), `payment_reference?`, `paid_at?` (inmutables post-confirmación). Interfaz `CheckoutProvider` agnóstica (`create_payment(order) → {reference, checkout_url}`; `get_payment(reference) → estado`; `verify_webhook(payload)`) + `MockCheckoutProvider` (IDs determinísticos, transiciones `pending → approved | rejected | failed`). Tests: idempotencia, replay, estados desordenados, cash permanece `paid=false`. Commit: `feat(orders): pagos Mercado Pago (CheckoutProvider + mock) + webhook idempotente`. [DONE: 46682b8, PR #27]
